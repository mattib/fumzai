# FumzAI

A 100% local, privacy-first browser extension that acts as your late-night bouncer for AI tools.

## The Problem (The Objection)
Traditional website blockers are trivial to bypass—especially when you're tired, slightly wired, and just want to ask ChatGPT "one last question." You know the passcode, you disable the extension, and suddenly it's 3 AM.

## The Solution
Instead of blocking the website itself, FumzAI recruits the AI to be the bouncer. 

FumzAI runs entirely locally in your browser. During your specified "curfew" hours, it intercepts your requests to tools like ChatGPT or Claude and secretly injects a system prompt instructing the AI to refuse to work, act like a tired bouncer, and tell you to go to sleep.

Because the AI itself is enforcing the boundary, it breaks the dopamine loop of mindlessly bypassing a standard website blocker.

### Core Values
- **100% Local:** No external servers.
- **Privacy-First:** Your chat data never leaves your machine.
- **Zero Analytics:** We don't track you.
